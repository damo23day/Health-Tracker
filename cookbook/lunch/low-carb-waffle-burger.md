# Low-Carb Waffle Burger

**Category:** Lunch / Dinner | **Status:** To try | **Servings:** 1 burger

## Original recipe (user supplied)
### Vegetable waffle buns
- 3 tbsp shredded zucchini, squeezed dry
- 2 tbsp shredded carrot
- 2 tbsp shredded mozzarella or similar cheese
- 1 large egg
- Salt, pepper; optional garlic powder

### Burger and sauce
- 1 beef mince patty (size and fat content unspecified)
- 1 cheese slice
- Lettuce, tomato, pickle
- Sauce: 2 tbsp Greek yoghurt + 1 tsp sriracha or hot sauce

### Original method
Mix bun ingredients, cook in a lightly greased mini waffle maker in two batches until crisp and golden (about 3–5 minutes each, depending on waffle maker). Cook beef patty thoroughly. Assemble with sauce, vegetables, patty, cheese and pickles between two waffles.

**User-supplied original macros (1 burger):** ~420–460 kcal; 28–32 g protein; 8–10 g carbs; 28–32 g fat; 2–3 g fibre. Unverified: patty weight, mince fat percentage and cheese quantities are not specified.

## Health Tracker version — 1 burger
- 150 g lean beef mince (5% fat), **raw weight**
- 45 g shredded zucchini, squeezed dry
- 25 g shredded carrot
- 20 g reduced-fat shredded mozzarella
- 1 large egg
- 20 g reduced-fat cheese slice
- 60 g combined lettuce, tomato and pickles
- 30 g plain Greek yoghurt
- 5 g sriracha
- Salt, pepper and optional garlic powder
- Light cooking oil spray

### Method
1. Thoroughly squeeze moisture from shredded zucchini.
2. Combine zucchini, carrot, mozzarella, egg and seasonings.
3. Preheat and lightly oil mini waffle maker. Cook mixture in two portions until set and golden. Let waffles rest briefly on a rack to preserve crispness.
4. Form and cook beef patty until safely cooked through (minced beef should reach 75°C at centre).
5. Stir yoghurt and sriracha together.
6. Assemble waffles with sauce, lettuce, tomato, beef, cheese and pickles. Serve immediately.

**~450 kcal | ~53 g protein | ~14 g carbs | ~19 g fat**. These are approximate planning estimates, not verified label calculations; actual values depend on brands, egg size, oil and mince.
