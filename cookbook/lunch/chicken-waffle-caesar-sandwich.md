# Chicken Waffle Caesar Sandwich

**Status:** Tested once — delicious flavour; waffle structure needs improvement | **Categories:** Lunch, Dinner | **Yield:** 2 sandwiches
**Source:** Facebook video by **Healthylifestyle**, supplied as screenshots by user (28 October 2026). Exact video URL not supplied.

## Original ingredients (2 sandwiches)
- 1½ cups shredded **cooked** chicken (weigh before cooking for reliable macros)
- 1 egg
- 50 g shredded cheese
- Salt and black pepper
- 2 tbsp cottage cheese (or spread of choice)
- Lettuce
- 4 tomato slices
- Caesar dressing (amount unspecified)

## Original method (from supplied screenshots)
1. Combine shredded cooked chicken, egg, grated cheese and black pepper in a bowl; season with salt if desired.
2. Portion mixture into a preheated waffle iron and cook until golden brown and crispy. The screenshot does not specify iron temperature or cooking time; ensure the egg mixture is fully cooked.
3. For each sandwich, layer cottage cheese, shredded lettuce, sliced tomatoes and Caesar dressing between two chicken waffles.
