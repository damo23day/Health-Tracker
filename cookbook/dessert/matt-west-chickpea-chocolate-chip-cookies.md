# Chickpea Chocolate Chip Cookies — Matt West “F*CK or SUCK” series

**Status:** MUST TRY / untested | **Category:** Dessert, Snack | **Yield:** 8 cookies
**Source:** Matt West, “F*CK or SUCK” chickpea cookies video (Facebook screenshot provided by user, 28 October 2026). Exact post URL not supplied. Series attribution is based on screenshot; not an endorsement or review by the user.

## Ingredients — as shown in screenshot
- 165 g cooked chickpeas (drained)
- 80 g peanut butter
- 30 g honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ¼ tsp baking powder
- 50 g dark chocolate chips

## Method — visible in screenshots
1. Blend chickpeas, peanut butter, honey, vanilla and baking powder until smooth.
2. Stir through chocolate chips.
3. Divide into 8 cookies on a lined baking tray and flatten slightly.
