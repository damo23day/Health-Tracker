# Chocolate Banana Mug Cake

## Health Tracker version
### Ingredients
- 1 small ripe banana (~100 g peeled)
- 1 large egg
- 8 g unsweetened cocoa powder
- 10 g peanut butter
- 1/4 tsp baking powder (optional, helps rise)
- Optional splash of milk

### Instructions
1. Mash banana thoroughly in a microwave-safe mug or small bowl.
2. Mix in egg, cocoa, peanut butter and optional baking powder until smooth.
3. Microwave approximately 60–90 seconds, then continue in 10–15 second bursts if needed until set throughout.
4. Rest for 1 minute before eating.
