# Warm Cinnamon Apple & Yoghurt

**ID:** warm-cinnamon-apple-yoghurt | **Category:** Dessert / Snack | **Status:** To try | **Servings:** 1

## Health Tracker version
### Ingredients
- 1 medium apple (~150 g), diced
- 150 g plain high-protein Greek yoghurt
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tbsp water
- Optional 1 tsp honey or chopped nuts (log separately)

### Instructions
1. Dice apple and place in a microwave-safe bowl with water and cinnamon.
2. Microwave 2–3 minutes, stir, then heat in short bursts until tender.
3. Allow to cool slightly and spoon yoghurt on top.
