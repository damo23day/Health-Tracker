# Chocolate Protein Mousse

**ID:** chocolate-protein-mousse | **Category:** Dessert / Snack | **Status:** Tried (27 October 2026) | **Servings:** 1

## Convenience rating
- **Total / active time:** 5 / 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** 1/5
- **Ingredient hassle:** 1/5
- **Taste:** Not rated (untested)

## Health Tracker version
### Ingredients
- 170 g plain high-protein Greek yoghurt
- 25 g chocolate whey protein
- 5 g unsweetened cocoa powder (optional)
- 1–2 tsp milk or water if needed
- Optional berries for serving

### Instructions
1. Stir yoghurt, whey and cocoa until smooth.
2. Add a splash of milk or water to adjust consistency.
3. Eat immediately or chill briefly for a thicker texture.

### Estimated nutrition (per serving)
Approximately **240 kcal** and **35 g protein**. Rough planning estimates only, based on generic ingredients; verify labels, weights and optional toppings before logging.
