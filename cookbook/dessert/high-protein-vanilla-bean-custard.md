# Homemade High-Protein Vanilla Bean Custard

**Version:** v1 — proposed, not yet cooked or rated  
**Category:** Dessert / snack  
**Yield:** 2 servings (about 170–180 g each)  
**Time:** About 10–15 minutes preparation/cooking plus 1–2 hours chilling  
**Inspiration:** Homemade alternative to Wicked Sister High-Protein Vanilla Bean Custard (170 g commercial tub). This is an original adaptation, not the manufacturer's recipe.

## Ingredients (whole batch)
- 300 mL skim milk
- 40 g vanilla whey protein powder (brand and nutrition to be confirmed)
- 12 g cornflour; increase to 15 g for a firmer set
- 1 tsp vanilla extract (or vanilla bean paste)
- Sweetener to taste, optional
- Small pinch of salt

## Method
1. Whisk **cold** skim milk and cornflour in a saucepan until smooth.
2. Stir constantly over medium-low heat until the starch cooks and the mixture visibly thickens; avoid scorching.
3. Remove from the heat and let cool slightly. Mix the protein powder with a small amount of the warm custard in a separate bowl to form a smooth paste, then whisk back into the pan.
4. Stir in vanilla, salt and optional sweetener. Avoid boiling after adding whey protein, which may become grainy.
5. Divide into two clean containers. Cool promptly and refrigerate for 1–2 hours until chilled and set. Keep refrigerated and consume within 2–3 days.

## Nutrition (per serving, provisional)
Approximately **150 kcal**, **22 g protein**, **12 g carbohydrates** and **2 g fat**. Values are estimates only and depend on the protein powder, milk and actual serving yield; recalculate from the chosen product labels before treating as verified.

## Substitutions and adjustments
- For a firmer, spoonable texture, use **15 g cornflour** instead of 12 g (adds roughly 5 kcal per serving).
- For a richer flavour, replace 50 mL skim milk with 50 mL full-cream milk; recalculate nutrition.
- Vanilla bean paste can replace extract; check added sugar on the label.
- Protein powder formulation matters: whey, casein and meal-replacement powders thicken differently. Adjust liquid or starch after the first test.
