# High-Protein Pistachio Cheesecake

**ID:** high-protein-pistachio-cheesecake | **Category:** Dessert / Snack | **Status:** To try | **Servings:** 6

**Source:** https://thechefoutwest.com/high-protein-pistachio-cheesecake/ — Chef Josh, The Chef Out West

## Original ingredients (6 servings)
- 225 g cottage cheese (approximately 1 US cup)
- 245 g Greek yoghurt (approximately 1 US cup)
- 2 large eggs
- 120 g shelled pistachios (approximately 1 US cup)
- 105 g maple syrup or 115 g honey (approximately 1/3 US cup)
- 14 g almond flour or 15 g oat flour (approximately 2 tbsp)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Optional extra chopped pistachios to garnish

## Preparation summary
1. Heat oven to 165°C and line a 15 cm (6-inch) round cake tin.
2. Blend cottage cheese, yoghurt, eggs, pistachios, sweetener, flour and vanilla thoroughly until smooth.
3. Pour into prepared tin; bake about 40–45 minutes until edges are set and centre still gently jiggles.
4. Cool, then refrigerate for at least 8 hours or overnight.
5. Slice into six servings; add optional chopped pistachios when serving.
