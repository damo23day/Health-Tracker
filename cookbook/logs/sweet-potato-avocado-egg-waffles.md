### Lessons / adjustments for next cook
1. Dry the sweet potato rather than compensating with extra flour.
2. Microwave without added water in a covered vented microwave-safe dish, let steam escape, drain if necessary.
3. Mix cheese and seasoning into sweet potato first; add 20 g flour initially and **gradually** add beaten egg to a thick scoopable consistency.
4. Spread smaller/thinner portions; brush both plates lightly with oil.
5. Test smoked paprika and garlic powder for flavour; add Tajín on toppings if desired.
