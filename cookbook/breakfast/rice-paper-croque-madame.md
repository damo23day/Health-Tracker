# Rice Paper Croque Madame

- **ID:** rice-paper-croque-madame
- **Categories:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner
- **Status:** To try; not personally rated
- **Servings:** 2 pockets (1 per person)
- **Total time:** 20–24 minutes; **prep:** 10 minutes
- **Estimated difficulty:** 2/5; **ingredient hassle:** 3/5
- **Source:** https://thechefoutwest.com/rice-paper-croque-madame/ (Chef Josh, The Chef Out West)

## Original recipe ingredients (2 pockets)
- 4 rice paper sheets
- 2 eggs whisked for dipping the rice paper
- 2 eggs for topping
- 6 thin slices of Black Forest ham
- 2 slices of cheese (e.g. Swiss, mozzarella, Gruyère)
- 2 tablespoons everything bagel seasoning
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- Light oil spray, salt and pepper

## Original method (summarised)
1. Preheat fan oven or air fryer to approximately 220°C.
2. Sprinkle seasoning on a board. Briefly dip two rice paper sheets in whisked egg, stacking them over the seasoning.
3. Spread Dijon in the middle, then layer cheese and three slices of ham.
4. Fold the four corners toward the centre to make a pocket, leaving the centre open.
5. Transfer to an oiled baking surface; crack an egg into the opening.
6. Repeat for the second pocket. Lightly season and cook approximately 10–14 minutes until crisp and the egg whites are set.
