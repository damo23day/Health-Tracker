# Sweet Potato, Avocado & Egg Waffles

**Category:** Breakfast | **Status:** To try | **Servings:** 1 (adapted version)

## Why it fits Health Tracker
Sweet potato supplies carbohydrate and fibre, avocado supplies unsaturated fats, and eggs provide protein. For a higher-protein breakfast, this version adds egg whites and limits the cheese and avocado portions.

## Ingredients — Health Tracker version (1 serving)
- 120 g cooked sweet potato, mashed
- 1 large egg (waffle mixture)
- 15 g reduced-fat grated cheese
- 100 g egg whites (waffle mixture)
- 40 g avocado, mashed (topping)
- 1 large egg (fried topping)
- Salt, pepper, chilli flakes
- Light cooking oil spray
- Optional sesame seeds (weigh if tracking)

## Method
1. Mash cooked sweet potato with one egg, egg whites, cheese, salt and pepper.
2. Preheat and lightly oil the waffle maker.
3. Cook mixture in batches until firm and golden. Extra egg whites make the mixture softer; cook thoroughly and avoid overfilling.
4. Mash avocado and spread on cooked waffles.
5. Cook the second egg and add on top.
6. Finish with chilli flakes and optional measured sesame seeds.
