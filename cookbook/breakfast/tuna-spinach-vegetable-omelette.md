# Tuna, Spinach & Vegetable Omelette

**Category:** Breakfast / Lunch | **Status:** To try | **Servings:** 1

## Original recipe (user supplied)
Ingredients: 2 tbsp finely grated carrot; 2 tbsp finely chopped broccoli; 3 large eggs; salt and black pepper; shredded mozzarella (quantity unspecified); handful of spinach; 2 tbsp canned or cooked tuna; thin red onion rings; oil for pan.

Method:
1. Whisk carrot, broccoli, eggs, salt and pepper.
2. Heat oil in non-stick pan; add mixture and cook until nearly set.
3. Flip briefly, then flip back.
4. Add mozzarella, spinach, tuna and onion to one half.
5. Fold and heat until cheese melts. Serve hot.

**Original nutrition:** Not provided. Indicative estimate using 30 g mozzarella, 30 g drained tuna and 5 g oil: ~405 kcal, ~33 g protein, ~7 g carbs, ~27 g fat. Estimate only.

## Health Tracker version (1 serving)
- 3 large eggs
- 20 g grated carrot
- 20 g finely chopped broccoli
- 25 g reduced-fat mozzarella
- 50 g canned tuna in springwater, drained
- 25 g fresh spinach
- 20 g red onion, thinly sliced
- Light oil spray
- Salt, pepper and optional chilli flakes

### Instructions
1. Prepare and weigh vegetables and drain tuna.
2. Lightly spray non-stick pan and soften broccoli, carrot and onion for 1–2 minutes.
3. Whisk eggs with seasoning; add to pan and cook gently until nearly set.
4. Add mozzarella, spinach and tuna to one half.
5. Fold, cover briefly and cook until egg is fully set, filling is heated and cheese melts.
